About the Portal

Thirty documents stand between a college and its Extension of Approval. This portal checks every one before an officer has to. Institutions upload on one side and hear at once what is missing, expired or inconsistent; officers open the same file on the other side with every finding tied to its evidence.

A document is marked Verified only where it agrees with the issuing authority's record. Where no source exists, the status stays Declared. Nothing here decides anything: risk scores order the work queue, and the decision is recorded by a Senior Officer under their own name.

Checks applied to each application

  1. M1 · 01The Bouncer. Incomplete files are turned back at upload with the exact fix — “signature not detected on the execution page” — while the college can still correct them.
  2. M2 · 02Room measurement. An officer picks a room; the college measures it live with a phone. Outside a 5% band against the building plan, the file says “inspect”, never “fraud”.
  3. M3 · 03Source comparison. Certificate numbers are read off the page and compared with the issuing authority’s record, field by field. Verified means the two records agree — nothing stronger is claimed.
  4. M4 · 04Editing indicators and image hashes. One weak signal can only raise a Low flag. A reused laboratory photo is still caught after resizing, at a Hamming distance of 3 in 64.
  5. M5 · 05Financial tests. Benford’s Law over the ledger — only when at least 100 numbers exist — beside the plainer test: does the FDR figure agree across bank letter, accounts and application.
  6. M6 · 06Faculty arithmetic. Degrees and payroll checked name by name; student–faculty and cadre ratios computed over verified teachers only, so dummy rows stop helping.
  7. M7 · 07Cross-college reuse. The same professor, land deed or photograph filed by two colleges surfaces as one linked picture.
