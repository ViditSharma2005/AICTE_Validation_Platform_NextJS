What was carried forward, and what was checked again

Its earlier verdict is carried forward and reported as auto-passed. Anything else is re-checked in full. Nothing is skipped that the cache cannot vouch for.

M5 Financial checks

First digits, and whether the FDR figures agree

Benford’s Law expects about 30% of honest ledger amounts to start with 1 and under 5% with 9. Invented figures drift toward the middle and end digits. The test needs volume, so below 100 numbers it stays silent rather than guess.

NET Network evidence — declared against observed devices

How many machines actually answer on campus

The campus polling agent records which machines answer on the college network over three days. Only a salted fingerprint of each hardware address reaches the server — raw MAC addresses are never stored or shown — so the same physical machine is recognisable at two colleges without exposing it. Device evidence tells an officer where to look; it is not proof of ownership.
